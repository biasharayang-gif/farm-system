What are you mixing?

Ingredient values (change if you tested them)

Defaults follow the Biashara Yangu feed guide: ochongaa 54% protein, maize 9% protein, Aminovit at 1 to 2 g per kg of feed (Aminovit Plus P label; always follow the label on your pack). The mix balances crude protein only. Check that the premix also supplies calcium, phosphorus and trace minerals; if not, add limestone/DCP or a mineral premix as advised. Ochongaa quality varies, so test it for sand and shell if you can. Enter your prices under Money to see the cost per kg.

Daily ratio by age

Grams of feed per bird per day. The rest of each mix is maize meal. Sample values to confirm against the Biashara Yangu feed guide.

Records

FCR is feed eaten divided by weight gained (kg of feed for 1 kg of gain). The book gives 2.5 to 3.0 as a good kienyeji figure. Weigh a sample of birds at the start and end of each period to work out the gain.
